Background

I hold a BS in Physics from the University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Lahore, where coursework spanned classical mechanics, electrodynamics, quantum mechanics, and statistical physics — alongside a growing focus on the computational side: solving physical problems numerically when closed-form answers run out.

That combination shapes how I build software today. I care about correctness the way a physics problem set demands it — checked units, verified limits, sane boundary conditions — applied to code instead of derivations.
